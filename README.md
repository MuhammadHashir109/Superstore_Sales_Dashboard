# 📊 SuperStore Sales Dashboard

![Dashboard]([1778219049840_image.png](https://github.com/MuhammadHashir109/Superstore_Sales_Dashboard/blob/main/Superstore%20Sales%20Dashboard.PNG)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **SuperStore Sales Dashboard** built using **Power BI**, analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and regional trends across multiple dimensions.

---

## 🔢 Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| 💰 Total Sales | $1.57M |
| 📈 Total Profit | $175K |
| 🚚 Avg Ship Days | 4 Days |
| 📊 Growth Rate (2019–2020) | 0.77 (77%) |

---

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Impressive YoY Growth
- Sales grew by an outstanding **77% from 2019 to 2020**, reflecting strong business momentum and effective sales strategies.

### 2️⃣ Payment Mode Preferences
- **COD dominates at 43%** — customers still prefer Cash on Delivery
- Online payments account for **35%**, showing a growing shift toward digital transactions
- Card payments make up the remaining **22%**

### 3️⃣ Customer Segments
- **Consumers** are the largest segment at **48%**
- **Corporate** follows at **33%**
- **Home Office** at **19%** — a segment with significant untapped growth potential

### 4️⃣ Regional Performance
| Region | Sales Share |
|---|---|
| West | 33% |
| East | 29% |
| Central | 22% |
| South | 16% |

> 📌 The **South region underperforms** — a major opportunity for regional expansion strategies.

### 5️⃣ Shipping Mode Analysis
| Ship Mode | Revenue |
|---|---|
| Standard Class | $912K |
| Second Class | $315K |
| First Class | $243K |
| Same Day | $96K |

> Customers strongly prefer **Standard Class**, prioritizing cost savings over faster delivery.

### 6️⃣ Category Performance
| Category | Sales |
|---|---|
| Office Supplies | $644K |
| Technology | $471K |
| Furniture | $452K |

> **Office Supplies** leads all categories, outperforming even Technology.

### 7️⃣ Top Sub-Categories
| Sub-Category | Sales |
|---|---|
| Phones | $0.20M |
| Chairs | $0.18M |
| Binders | $0.17M |

### 8️⃣ Seasonal Trends
- Both **Sales and Profit show a strong Q4 surge**, indicating seasonal demand peaks.
- This insight is valuable for planning **targeted marketing campaigns** during peak months.
